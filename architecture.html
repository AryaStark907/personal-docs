STEP 1
📐
Metric Aggregation
metrics.py
Splits data into prev/curr week. Calculates WoW % changes. Computes funnel rates.
→
STEP 2
🚨
Problem Detection
metrics.py
Finds the biggest negative change across all metrics. Identifies affected funnel stage.
→
STEP 3
🔬
Root Cause Analysis
analysis.py
Generates 3–5 hypotheses for the problem. Ranks by likelihood.
STEP 4
🌍
Benchmarking
analysis.py
Maps problem to how Duolingo, Netflix, Spotify, TikTok & Roblox solved it.
→
STEP 5
🧪
Experiment Generation
analysis.py
Creates 4 A/B test ideas inspired by benchmarks. Each has a target metric & success threshold.
→
STEP 6–7
📈
Impact & Prioritisation
analysis.py
Scores each experiment: Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Ranks 1–4.
→
STEP 8
🏆
Recommendation
agent.py
Selects top experiment. States expected lift, timeline, and key risk.
